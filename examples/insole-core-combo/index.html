ORPHE INSOLE + ORPHE CORE Simultaneous Connection
Version 1.0

This tool acquires synchronized data from two ORPHE INSOLE units (left/right) and one ORPHE CORE on a single page. Turn on each toggle in the header to open the device chooser: devices starting with "INS" appear in the ORPHE INSOLE dialogs, and devices starting with "CR-" in the ORPHE CORE dialog.

On the foot maps, higher load is shown in red, and marks the estimated center of pressure (CoP) computed as the weighted average of the six sensor points.
